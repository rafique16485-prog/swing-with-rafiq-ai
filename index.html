Lesson 1 — What is Option Trading?

ELI5: An option is a contract whose value changes with an underlying asset such as NIFTY. A CALL generally benefits from a rise in the underlying; a PUT generally benefits from a fall.

Real example: If NIFTY is 25,000 and a 25,000 CE premium is ₹100, the premium may rise or fall depending on price movement, time, volatility and other factors.

DO: Understand the underlying, direction, strike, expiry, premium and risk before trading.

DON'T: Treat options like a lottery, buy random cheap premiums, average losses, or revenge trade.

Psychology: Your job is not to predict every move. Your job is to follow a repeatable process and protect capital.
